# How to redeem Duolingo Super step by step

**Last checked against the official page:** 14 October 2026, on [Duolingo's help page](https://www.duolingo.com/help/use-a-promo-code). We re-check it every 30 days or whenever the interface changes.

## Before you start

- Make sure you do not have an active Super subscription. According to Duolingo, a code cannot be used on an active subscription.
- You must be at least 13 years old.
- Have a web browser ready. According to Duolingo, redemption currently works from a browser only.

## Redemption steps

1. Open duolingo.com/redeem in a web browser (this works on a phone, tablet or computer).
2. Enter the code and press "Redeem", then press "Claim offer" if it shows you are eligible.
3. Sign in to your account or create one, choose the plan and follow the screens until Super starts.

## On a phone or tablet

Redemption happens in the browser, even when you are on a phone. Once you are subscribed, Super features appear in the iOS and Android apps and on the web.

## Common errors and fixes

| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Promo code invalid | The code expired, was already used, or was typed incorrectly | Retype it carefully and make sure you are signed in |
| The code is not accepted | You have an active Super subscription, or you are under 13 | According to Duolingo, a code cannot be applied to an active subscription |
| The code fails while you are in a classroom | Your account is linked to a Duolingo classroom | Leave the classroom, then enter the code again |
| The link never arrived | The email was mistyped or went to spam | Open your order page and correct the email, or contact us with your order reference |

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the subscription renew automatically?**
According to Duolingo, all subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel them.

**Can I use the link on an account that already has a subscription?**
No. A code cannot be used on an active Super subscription.

**Will my progress or streak be affected?**
According to Duolingo, your progress and streak are not affected when you start or end a subscription.
