# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026.

We collect as little data as we can and use it only to deliver and support your order.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address (entered after payment) | To send your redeem link and answer your requests |
| Order reference, market and amount | To manage the order and match the payment |
| Payment transaction details, such as a transaction ID | To confirm payment and prevent it being used twice |
| IP address | To prevent abuse and limit request rates |
| Display name and rating, if you write a review | To show the review next to the order's country |

We never ask for your Duolingo password or for bank card details.

## How we use it

To deliver your order, send transactional messages (your link and support replies), send one review invitation you can ignore, prevent fraud and keep the site secure, and meet accounting and legal duties.

## Cookies and analytics

We use analytics that do not rely on cookies, and we place no marketing cookies or tracking pixels in our emails. If we add non-essential cookies later, we will ask for your consent first.

## Who we share it with

Only the providers we need: our email provider, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare), and the service or API used to detect your payment. We do not sell your data.

## Security

Email addresses are stored encrypted, admin access is protected by two-factor authentication, and sensitive actions are logged.

## How long we keep it

We keep order data for as long as accounting, legal duties and support require, then delete it or make it anonymous.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by emailing support@lingora.world. We verify you through the email linked to the order. Depending on your country you may have further rights under local law.

## Children

The service is not aimed at anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above. Questions: support@lingora.world. See also our [refund policy](/en/refund-policy/).
