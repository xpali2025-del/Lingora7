## The secret of our edge

Our edge is not a slogan; it is small details that add up. We build each page to answer one question precisely, we check official sources and print the last-verified date beside anything that can change, and an automated similarity check stops us publishing near-duplicate pages. Orders run on a system that prevents a payment being counted twice or a link being delivered twice, so your result does not depend on luck. What you get is what competing pages rarely give: a complete activation guide with fixes for its errors, and a straight answer on what a subscription does and does not cover.
