# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026.

The product is a digital redeem link, delivered to your email as soon as payment is confirmed and usable once. For that reason we do not offer refunds after delivery simply because you changed your mind. We do help in the cases below.

## When we replace a link or refund you

| Situation | What we do |
|---|---|
| You paid but the link never arrived | We resend it, and refund you if we cannot deliver |
| The link does not work, or was already used before it reached you | We replace it, and refund you if none is available |
| You paid but no order was created, or it had expired | We review the payment and create the order or refund you |
| You overpaid | We refund the difference or credit it, as you prefer |
| You underpaid | We ask you to send the difference, or refund what you paid |

## What is not covered

- Your Duolingo account being ineligible, for example because you already have an active Super subscription. Duolingo says a code cannot be used on an active subscription, so check your account before buying.
- Funds sent on a network other than BEP20 or to a wrong address. We may not be able to recover them.
- A link that was successfully activated on your account.

## How to make a request

Email support@lingora.world with your order reference, a short description, and a screenshot of any message you saw. We reply with our decision, and pay any refund within a reasonable time of approval.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid in USDT on the BEP20 network to an address you provide, and network fees may be deducted. We do not refund in other currencies or by bank transfer. Check the address carefully, because transactions cannot be undone.
