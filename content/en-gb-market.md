## Duolingo Super for holidays: a twelve-week plan

Many UK learners start a few months before a trip and stall by week three. This plan is only a suggestion, and the point is consistency rather than speed:

1. **Weeks 1 to 2:** five to ten minutes a day on the commute, focusing on greetings, numbers and ordering food.
2. **Weeks 3 to 6:** add a daily sentence you would really say on holiday, such as asking for the bill, and say it aloud.
3. **Weeks 7 to 10:** use the mistakes practice that Super includes, according to Duolingo, to revisit the words you keep getting wrong.
4. **Weeks 11 to 12:** read a menu or a station sign in the target language and see how much you understand without help.

## Returning after school languages
